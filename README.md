# ⚡ Operator AI v4.0 — Autonomous Business Operating System

An enterprise-grade, autonomous revenue and operations director for D2C & Shopify brands.

---

## 🌟 Workspaces & Core Features

1. **⚡ Overview (`/`)**:
   - Executive Briefing & Yesterday's Performance (Sales, 31% Net Take-Home Profit, 2.98x Ad Return).
   - Recommended Actions with 1-Click HITL (Human-in-the-Loop) execution.
   - **Verified ROI Impact Ledger**: Quantified financial value (+₹1,42,800 net value created, ₹48,200 ad waste prevented, ₹94,600 WhatsApp carts recovered).
   - Sales vs Ad Spend & Lost Cart Trends.

2. **💰 Profit & Units (`/profit`)**:
   - **Interactive "What-If?" Profit & Scale Simulator**: Dynamic sliders (Ad Spend, Target ROAS, AOV, COGS %) with live 30-day projection, Breakeven CAC, and inventory stockout threat warnings.
   - 1-Click **Set as AI Target Strategy**.
   - P&L Cost Waterfall (Where every ₹100 of revenue goes).
   - Product-Level Margin Matrix with AI Flagging (*High Profit Winners*, *Margin Squeeze*, *Losing Money on Ads*).

3. **🚀 Growth & Recovery (`/growth`)**:
   - **Meta Ads & Creative Fatigue Auditor**: 3s Attention Grab %, Audience Freshness (FTIR), and Frequency.
   - **AI Creative Studio & UGC Hook Generator**: 3 proven video hooks (*Problem-Agitation*, *Social Proof*, *Behind the Scenes*), 20s script breakdown, and ready Meta ad copy.
   - **Abandoned Cart Recovery**: Real-time 42 drop-off feed (₹88,400 value), interactive iPhone WhatsApp simulator, and 1-click **Recover All Carts** blast.
   - **One-Click WhatsApp VIP Retention**: Pre-built customer segments (*Lapsed VIPs*, *Serum Repeat Buyers*) with dynamic discount generators.

4. **🧠 Brain & Rules (`/rules`)**:
   - **Autopilot vs. Co-Pilot Control Switchboard**: 3-way autonomy switches (`[ OFF ]`, `[ ASK ME FIRST (Co-Pilot) ]`, `[ ⚡ FULL AUTOPILOT ]`) across 4 critical workflows.
   - **Founder Safety Guardrails**: Active constraints (*Weekend Spend Caps*, *18% Margin Safeguards*) with compliance sandbox.
   - **Days of Stock Left & Restock Rules**: Days of Supply Remaining (DoSR) countdown and 1-click supplier PO dispatch.
   - **Connected Apps Hub**: Shopify, Meta Ads, GA4, WhatsApp, Klaviyo connections.

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **UI & Styling**: React 18, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Framer Motion
- **Theme Engine**: `next-themes` (Light, Dark, System)
- **Charts & Feedback**: Recharts, Sonner Toasts
- **Database Support**: MongoDB Atlas (Dedicated Database: `operator_ai_db`) / Reactive In-Memory Store
